Privacy Policy

Devil's Codename (iPhone app and web version) · Last updated November 22, 2026

Devil's Codename is a word game. It has no accounts, no ads and no tracking, and it does not sell or share your data. This page explains the little data the game needs to work.

What the game sends to our server

  • Online rooms: the name you type, the room code, and your moves (the secret word you pick, your hints and guesses) go to our game server so the other players in your room can see the game. A random player token keeps your seat if you reconnect.
  • Hint scoring: on iPhone, hints are scored on your phone. When a score is a close call, the secret word and the hint (just those two words) are sent to our server for a second opinion. In the web version, the server scores every hint the same way.

Who else sees it

For close calls, our server asks Claude, an AI model made by Anthropic, how strongly the two words are related. Only the two words are sent, with nothing that identifies you. Anthropic processes them under its commercial terms and does not use them to train its models. No other third parties receive game data.

How long we keep it

  • Rooms, including player names and moves, are deleted after 3 hours without activity.
  • Word pairs that were scored (for example "road" and "highway") are kept, with no names or other details, so the same pair scores the same way next time.
  • Server logs are kept briefly for running and fixing the service.

On your device

The app stores your name, your preferred difficulty and your current room on your device so you don't have to re-enter them. Deleting the app removes them. It also keeps a count of how many games your phone has hosted this month, for the free hosting limit; that count stays on the phone.

Purchases

Devil's Pass and tips are paid through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment; we never see your card details or Apple ID, only that a purchase was made.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Players can use any nickname.

Changes and contact
