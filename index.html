How to play

  • Everyone picks a secret word from the five they're dealt.
  • On your turn, give hints of 1 to 3 words. The AI scores how strongly each hint points to your word.
  • When a hint reaches the bar (80, 90 or 95%), everyone else gets one guess. The first right guess scores a point.
  • If nobody guesses it, you score the point instead and keep hinting. So the trick is to convince the AI while fooling your friends.

Common questions

How do I play with friends online?

Tap Host a game, then share the 4-letter room code. Friends join from the app, or from any browser at devils-wordgame.fly.dev.

Can we play on one phone?

Yes. Choose Pass and play on the home screen and hand the phone around.

Is it free?

Joining games is always free, and each phone can host 100 games a month for free. The Devil's Pass is a one-time purchase that removes the hosting limit. Tips are optional and unlock nothing.

I bought the Devil's Pass on another device.

Open Support the game in the app and tap Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

The first hint takes a moment.

The scoring model loads on your phone the first time you play. After that, scoring is quick.
